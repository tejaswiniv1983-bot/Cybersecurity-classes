# Day 09 – Ethical Hacking Basics

## Objective
Understand ethical hacking and its role in improving security.

## Topics Covered
- Definition of ethical hacking
- Legal authorization
- Scope and rules of engagement
- Reconnaissance
- Security testing
- Reporting and remediation

## Key Learnings
Ethical hacking involves authorized security testing to identify weaknesses before malicious attackers exploit them.

Typical stages include:
1. Planning and obtaining permission.
2. Information gathering.
3. Identifying possible weaknesses.
4. Testing within the approved scope.
5. Documenting findings.
6. Recommending remediation.

## Ethical Guidelines
- Obtain written authorization.
- Follow the approved scope.
- Protect confidential information.
- Avoid disrupting systems.
- Report findings responsibly.

## Practical Work
Document authorized lab activities and the lessons learned.

## Conclusion
Learned the importance of permission, responsible testing, and security reporting.
