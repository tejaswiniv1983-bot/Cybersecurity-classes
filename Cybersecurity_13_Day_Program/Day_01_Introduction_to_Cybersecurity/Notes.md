- Common Cybersecurity Threats
- Basic Security Practices

## Key Learnings
Cybersecurity protects computers, networks, applications, and data from unauthorized access, attacks, damage, and theft.

The CIA Triad includes:
- **Confidentiality:** Ensuring information is accessible only to authorized people.
- **Integrity:** Ensuring information remains accurate and is not changed without authorization.
- **Availability:** Ensuring systems and information are accessible when needed.
