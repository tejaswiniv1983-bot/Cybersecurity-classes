# Day 07 – Phishing and Social Engineering

## Objective
Recognize social engineering attacks and learn how to avoid them.

## Topics Covered
- Phishing emails
- Smishing (SMS phishing)
- Vishing (voice phishing)
- Impersonation
- Suspicious links and attachments
- Security awareness

## Key Learnings
Social engineering manipulates people into revealing information or performing unsafe actions.

Common warning signs include:
- Unexpected requests for passwords or OTPs.
- Urgent or threatening messages.
- Suspicious links and attachments.
- Misspelled domains and impersonated senders.
- Requests for confidential information.

## Prevention
Verify requests through trusted channels, avoid suspicious links, enable MFA, and report suspected phishing.

## Practical Work
Document any phishing-awareness exercises completed in class. Use safe examples without real credentials.

## Conclusion
Learned how to identify suspicious messages and protect personal and organizational information.
